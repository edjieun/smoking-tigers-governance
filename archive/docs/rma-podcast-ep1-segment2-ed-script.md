## Topic 4 — Smoking Tigers Media
*(Ed's ~7 min block in Segment 2)*

### What it is
- A media company built on top of the regenerative economy and the communities building it.
- YouTube-first. Podcasts, conversations, event recordings. This show is the first product.
- Also the **ops and coordination layer** for what's happening here — contracts, production infrastructure, partnerships. The boring stuff that makes the interesting stuff happen.
- *[AD-LIB: "I didn't set out to be an ops guy, but here we are"]*

### The problem it solves
- The ecosystem has content. It doesn't have a central voice — no channel saying: here's what's happening, here's who's building what, here's why it matters.
- Sage has been in rooms talking about this for years. Those conversations don't exist outside those rooms.
- Smoking Tigers wants to **collect** what's already alive and make it visible, searchable, shareable.
- *[AD-LIB: Camp Audax — plan is to record 20–30 conversations in one week. "That's not a pipeline problem. That's a treasure chest problem."]*

### The model
- YouTube channel + partnership program → ad revenue. Not the point, but it funds the mission.
- Mutual amplification: you come on, you share something real, we give you reach. Our community amplifies you back.
- We invite orgs, collectives, GRCs — not just individuals. "You're not asking me, you're asking the thousand people I'm connected to."

### Where we are / where we need help
- The channel exists. The production process exists. Infrastructure is real.
- What's missing: **scale**. Small audience. Thin content library.
- Looking for: production help (editing, audio, visual identity), practitioners who want a home for their work, organizational partners with audiences.
- *[AD-LIB: be specific about what you can't do — "I am not a video editor and I have no shame about that"]*

## Topic 11 — RMA Member Project Spotlight / Shared Resources
*(Segment 2 add-on, or seed for a future episode)*

### Why shared resources matter
- Most people building in the regenerative space are running on individual subscriptions, personal email, whatever free tier they can find. That's a **tax on the mission**.
- If you're serious about community media, you need shared infrastructure — something you can hand off, that other members can log into and keep running.
- *[AD-LIB: the biggest bottleneck isn't ideas — it's tools and who knows how to use them]*

### What Quorum1 provides
- Q1 is a decentralized professional network. Ed is a steward. It provides: `@quorum.one` email, shared Notion, Firebase backend, GovRepo (versioned governance records).
- Smoking Tigers launched directly on Q1 tools. Everything here — the podcast, the tracking, the agent pipelines — runs on Q1 infrastructure.
- ST took Q1's GovRepo and iterated on it. That's the pattern: take what's built, use it, improve it.
- *[AD-LIB: name the specific things you use week to week]*

### The AI infrastructure layer (what ST adds)
- On top of Q1 ops tools, ST is adding shared AI: on-device models, OpenRouter access, agent pipelines.
- Workflow: AI drafts in markdown → Notion for review → Google Docs for co-editing. Rough ideas in, clean docs out.
- The goal: community members never have to pay for AI subscriptions individually again.
- *[AD-LIB: "That sounds like a fantasy. It's not. It's just what on-premise infrastructure looks like when you actually build it."]*

### The bigger point
- You don't need to hire a CTO. You plug into shared infrastructure and use the pattern.
- Victor's framing: *"We don't need these people to be bottlenecks. We just need to show people a new pattern to launch enterprises."*
- This is the Smoking Tigers offer: not just media, but the **ops layer**. Show communities how to launch with minimal friction.
- *[AD-LIB: "The thing we're doing right now — this podcast — is the output. The infrastructure behind it is the lesson."]*

### Questions to open up with Sage
1. "Sage, what tools are you using day-to-day to keep CARE Networks running? What's the gap?"
2. "If a bioregional org came to us tomorrow and said 'we need to be operational in 30 days' — what would we actually hand them?"
3. "Is shared infrastructure a prerequisite for building a bioregional media network, or can we fake it with individual subscriptions long enough to get traction?"
