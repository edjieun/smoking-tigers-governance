## Segment 1 — Intro to RMA: Why Are We Here?

**Lead:** Sage Harper
**Goal:** Viewers understand what RMA is, why it exists, and why these two people are hosting it. Creates curiosity to watch more.

**Opening hook:**
> "We're here to talk about a variety of subjects you'd find within a bioregion — and we're figuring out what that means together."

**Key talking points:**
1. What is RMA? — A peer-to-peer, low-complexity, trust-based network exploring regenerative media
2. What is bioregionalism and why does media matter to it?
3. Who are the hosts and why are they doing this? (Ed → Smoking Tigers; Sage → CARE Networks)
4. What does "success" look like for this show? — Engaged viewers asking questions, creating curiosity and desire to participate

**Sage's framing from planning call:**
> "I wasn't coming on to showcase my project. I was coming on to start talking about a variety of topics."
> "One block needs to be about RMA overall."

**Closing / handoff to Segment 2:**
> "Now that you know why we're here — let me tell you what we're each actually building."

## Segment 2 — What We're Working On

**Lead:** Ed + Sage (equal split — ~7 min each)
**Goal:** Viewers get a concrete sense of the work each host is doing and how it connects to the RMA mission.

**Opening:**
> "Half the time talking about Ed's project, half the time talking about Sage's project." (Sage, planning call)

**Ed's segment (Smoking Tigers):**
- What is Smoking Tigers Media?
- What problems in the current media system is it trying to solve?
- How does it connect to RMA and bioregionalism?
- Where do we need help / what's the current state?

**Sage's segment (CARE Networks):**

**Closing:**
