## 3. Plan Content & Copy First (Content-First Design)

A common mistake is designing layout boxes and trying to stuff words into them later. Content should drive the design, not the other way around.

- **Audit Existing Assets:** Do you have high-res photos, brand logos, testimonials, and copy ready?
    
- **Draft Key Copy Early:** Outline headline hooks, call-to-action (CTA) text, and key value propositions for main pages.
    
- **Gather Proof Points:** Collect reviews, case studies, client logos, and certifications to place strategically.

## 4. Visual Layout & Wireframing

Wireframes are stripped-down, black-and-white blueprints of your pages. They focus purely on structure and user experience (UX) without getting distracted by fonts and colors.

|**Phase**|**Tool Options**|**Focus**|
|---|---|---|
|**Low-Fidelity**|Pen & Paper, Excalidraw, Balsamiq|Basic layout, section ordering, CTAs.|
|**High-Fidelity**|Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch|Pixel-perfect UI, brand colors, typography, mobile responsiveness.|

> **Pro Tip:** Always plan for mobile devices first or simultaneously. Over 50% of web traffic comes from mobile phones—if it looks broken on a phone, it's broken.
